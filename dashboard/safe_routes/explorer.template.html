How the engine scores a route

Each street segment gets a risk between 0 and 1 for the chosen hour. Lighting only counts after dark. Unknown data is penalised, never assumed safe.

The engine runs A* nine times with the risk weight λ rising from 0 to 20. λ = 0 gives the fastest route. Among candidates within the detour budget, the lowest length-weighted risk is safest; the knee of the time-versus-risk trade-off is balanced. Safety index = 100 × (1 − mean risk).
